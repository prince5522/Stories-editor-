ℹ️ What is included?

These backups collect records managed by the app's local storage engine, including stories, episodes, planner records, settings and other saved sections.

Separate binary media files stored outside those records may not be included yet. Keep original photos, videos and documents separately until media cloud syncing has been implemented and tested.
